# Moonshot wishlist

Big, risky ideas that could change what Claudenspiel is. Each needs a prototype before it earns a place on the other lists.

- **A living library.** The game rebalances itself: after every few hundred online (or Genius) games, cards that win too often lose a point and cards that never win gain one, within limits, with every change logged as a patch note written by the game itself.
- **The Genius as a teacher.** At any moment, ask "what would the Genius do?" and see its move with a plain-language reason drawn from its search ("this claim wins the race by a turn"). Over a campaign, the hints fade as you improve.
- **Card generation.** A card forge that proposes new cards (name, suit, rules, cost), simulates each with bots before showing it, and lets the player keep the ones that fit. The best community cards join a folio.
- **Legacy campaign.** A campaign whose games change the deck for good: cards get stickers (upgrades, scars), a city district is founded or burned, a Foundation is lost. Played over 10–15 sessions with the same group.
- **Asymmetric boss mode.** One player is the Dragon, with its own deck and rules (it doesn't build a Kingdom; it scorches the board), against two to four players cooperating.
- **A board that is a place.** Instead of an endless grid, a fixed map of the prairie, river and walled city, with terrain that matters: the river blocks placement, hills see further, the city is worth double.
- **Real-time variant.** No turns: everyone plays at once with a ● trickle and cooldowns. Chaotic, quick, a party mode.
- **Physical edition.** Print-and-play PDF generated from the card data, with the Dithry art, so the game can be played at a table.
