# Gameplay wishlist

What Claudenspiel's rules and cards should become. ✅ done (with the release), ▶ next up, ○ later. The evidence behind most items is in [JOURNAL.md](../JOURNAL.md) and Kartenspiel's card atlas.

## Balance
- ✅ 0.1 A 116-card deck: staples doubled or tripled, the strongest and strangest cards single, every suit 18–20 cards, Ruin cards scaled so War stays as common.
- ✅ 0.1 Orchard 3◇ a card (Gardener was the strongest archetype in every test).
- ✅ 0.1 Observatory +10◇ when all six suits are in the Kingdom (Philosopher was the weakest; 6◇ a suit as well overshot).
- ✅ 0.2 Obelisk: cost 18, 6◇ doubled if surrounded (was 16 and 8◇). Card-level effect not yet measured; needs a per-card study.
- ▶ Cathedral: the Archbishop is still below par. Try 6◇ a Sceptre card plus 2◇ for each Sceptre card on the board that you don't own (mirrors Palace).
- ✅ 0.2 Fountain: 3◇ per adjacent Leaf (a tune, not a rework; re-measure, and rework if it is still last).
- ✅ 0.2 Death Ray: 9● for 3◇.
- ○ Re-run the Genius-only balance study after every balance release.

## Economy (● barely matters today: one claim a turn is the real limit, and about half of all ● goes unspent)
- ▶ A second claim: once a turn, pay 10● to claim one more card.
- ▶ Banking: at the end of your turn, each full 6● you didn't spend becomes a 1◇ token (at most 2 a turn).
- ○ Upkeep: cards costing 15● or more cost 2● a turn, or they flip face down (no ◇) until paid.
- ○ A market row: three face-up cards beside the deck; buy one into your hand for its cost minus 3.

## Turn structure and pacing
- ✅ 0.1 The last round: reaching the target starts it, everyone gets the same number of turns, the highest total wins.
- ✅ 0.1 Seat start off by default (with equal turns it double-paid later seats).
- ▶ Seat 1 in 3–4 player games may still be weak (0.61 in a small sample): measure with more games, then fix if real.
- ○ Catch-up draw: 15◇ or more behind the leader, draw 1 extra card at the start of your turn.
- ○ Meeple recall for 4● at the start of a turn, instead of costing the whole turn.

## New card types and cards
- ✅ 0.1 Revenge and Repent can also be played from hand (draw 2, discard 1); Brambles can be placed like any Structure.
- ▶ Grounds, the empty card type: terrain placed in empty spaces, never claimed, changing the cards next to it. First three: Marsh (Leaf: neighbours need one more enemy to be Overwhelmed), Crossroads (Goblet: placing next to it costs 3● less), Battlefield (Sword: Ruins aimed next to it cost nothing).
- ▶ You'll Regret That: give it a normal play too, so no card is ever dead in hand.
- ○ Cartographer (Tome Structure, 9●): look at the top 3 cards of the deck and put them back in any order.
- ○ Pilgrim's Road (Sceptre Blessing, 6●): a card of yours can't be Ruined until your next turn, and claiming next to it costs no meeple.
- ○ Feast Day (Goblet Action, 6●): everyone draws 1; you draw 2 and bank 1◇ for each Goblet card on the board.
- ○ Ossuary Bell (Bone Structure, 8●): whenever any card is buried anywhere, bank 1◇.
- ○ A seventh suit. Candidates: Coin (trade and debts) or Star (prophecy: cards that score at game end). Needs a seventh archetype.

## Interaction
- ○ Shared claims: claim a card someone already holds by paying its cost; both score it at half value.
- ○ Treaties: two players agree not to Ruin each other's cards for a round; breaking it costs ◇. Gives 3–5 player games diplomacy.
- ○ Hidden objectives: one each, worth 6◇ at the end (e.g. "own 4 suits", "Ruin 3 cards").
